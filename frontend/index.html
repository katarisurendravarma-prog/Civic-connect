Help & Customer Support

❓ How do I file a new complaint?

Go to the "Register Complaint" page, fill in the title, description, choose a category or select AI auto-detect, drop location coordinates on the map, and click submit.

❓ Can I edit my complaint details after submission?

No, once submitted, complaints are immediately routed. If you need to withdraw it, you can delete it using the "Delete" option from the tracking screen.
